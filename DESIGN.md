# Design direction — Anchor Aquatics

## Subject, audience, and job

- Subject: Pool management, maintenance, and construction in Edgewater, Maryland.
- Audience: local residents and property or business owners seeking the verified services listed on the current site.
- Single page job: make the verified phone and service fit obvious without inventing any business claim.

## Tokens

- Ink `#052C46`
- Mid `#087DA4`
- Soft `#6ED6E7`
- Accent `#FF6B35`
- Paper `#F4FBFD`
- Display: Georgia; body: Verdana.

## Layout and signature

Desktop uses a two-part hero, a verified-facts rail, a responsive service grid, an image-led business story, and a direct contact card. Mobile collapses to one column and gives the phone action full-width treatment.

Signature: A precise lane-line motif organizes commercial, residential, and employment paths.

## Uniqueness critique

The first plan risked becoming a generic local-service landing page. It was revised around a subject-specific structural device—a precise lane-line motif organizes commercial, residential, and employment paths. The palette is derived from the business's existing imagery or visual identity rather than a default cream/terracotta or black/acid scheme.

## Production behavior

- WCAG 2.2 AA intent: native links/buttons, visible focus, skip link, semantic landmarks, and reduced-motion handling.
- Global visible scrollbar styling and forced-colors fallback.
- Mobile menu focuses the first link, closes on Escape or outside click, and restores trigger focus on Escape.
- Image geometry is reserved with intrinsic dimensions and CSS aspect ratios.
- No forms are published; the only contact actions are the verified public phone and, when published, email.
